# Login

How to access and log in to the Ftrack admin portal at [ftrack.vn](https://ftrack.vn).

## Access the System

<!-- TODO: screenshot - ftrack.vn homepage with Login button -->

**Step 1:** Open your browser and go to [https://ftrack.vn](https://ftrack.vn)

**Step 2:** Click **Login** in the top-right corner

## Enter Your Credentials

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Login form with email and password fields -->

**Step 3:** Enter your **Email** and **Password**

**Step 4:** Click **Log In**

!!! warning "Note"
    Credentials are issued by your system admin. If you don't have an account, contact your organization's administrator.

## Forgot Password

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Forgot password link on the login form -->

**Step 1:** Click **Forgot Password** on the login page

**Step 2:** Enter your account email address

**Step 3:** Check your inbox and follow the instructions in the email

## After Logging In

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Dashboard after successful login -->

After logging in, you will see the Ftrack main dashboard with the left navigation bar and core features.
