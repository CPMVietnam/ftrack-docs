# Performing a Survey

How to complete the survey questionnaire after successfully checking in at a store.

## Open the Survey

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Survey screen with question list -->

After Check-In, the app automatically opens the Survey linked to the Visit Plan.

## Answer Questions

### Yes/No Questions

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Yes/No question in the app -->

Tap **Yes** or **No**

### Single-Choice Questions

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Single choice question in the app -->

Tap the answer you want to select (only one allowed)

### Multiple-Choice Questions

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Multiple choice question in the app -->

Tap multiple answers, then tap **Next**

### Number Input Questions

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Number input question in the app -->

Enter a numeric value using the keypad

### Text Input Questions

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Text input question in the app -->

Type your response in the text field

### Photo Capture Questions

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Photo capture question in the app -->

**Step 1:** Tap **Take Photo**

**Step 2:** Capture the photo as instructed (angle, product, shelf display...)

**Step 3:** Review and tap **Confirm** or **Retake**

## Required Questions

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Required question indicator in the app -->

Questions marked with **\*** are required — you must answer them before checking out.

## Save Draft and Continue Later

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Save draft button -->
<!-- TODO: verify - can surveys be saved as drafts -->

If you need to pause, tap **Save Draft**. Your entered data will be preserved.

## Complete the Survey

<!-- TODO: screenshot - Survey completion screen with Check Out button -->

After answering all questions, tap **Complete** to proceed to [Check Out](check-out.md).
